Model assumptions & sources

The DXF uses metres. Its vertical tie to the local tide gauge is unknown. Today is a 2026 design baseline at CAD Z = 0.00 m. Future offsets follow an illustrative accelerating path from zero to +0.65 m by 2100. For 2150–2500, the design test adds 1 cm per year after 2100, holding the mapped terrain and buildings fixed. These long-range levels are illustrative assumptions. Each tidal test adds the same offset in every year. Relative rise already includes land movement conceptually.

The water overlay follows low terrain connected to a mapped waterway across mesh edges. Isolated depressions stay dry. Canal contact is sampled from the supplied water meshes. The mapped canal layer follows the supplied mesh. Flow, drainage, MOSE operation, tides in real time and future construction are outside this model. Terrain triangles span about 20 m and cannot resolve individual thresholds. The route is a survey camera with ground following and building collision. Water can be crossed for inspection.

Published Venice projections have different baselines and emissions assumptions. The intermediate years here are design tests. Zanchettin et al., 2021 · Venice sea-level variability · Lagoon tidal range.
